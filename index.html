04 / case study

Sentinel

A security installation company was tracking customer sites, equipment and access credentials across scattered notes and memory. I built them a private operations platform that puts every site, device and encrypted credential behind one login.

It's in daily use by their team — techs look up a site on the road instead of phoning the office.
